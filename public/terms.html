GOOD TO KNOW

Simple rules.
Good experience.

These terms help us keep appointments running smoothly and make sure every client gets the House of Fades experience they came for.

01

Appointments

Appointments can be booked through our online booking system. Clients should provide accurate contact details when making a booking.

02

Cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please contact us as soon as possible. We appreciate reasonable notice so that the appointment slot can be offered to another client.

03

Late Arrivals

Please arrive on time for your appointment. Arriving late may reduce the time available for your service or require the appointment to be rescheduled.

04

Services & Pricing

Service prices are displayed on our website and may be updated from time to time. The price shown when booking represents the expected price of the selected service.

05

Client Information

Information provided during booking is used to manage your appointment and communicate important booking information. We do not ask for information that is unnecessary for providing our services.

06

Shop Conduct

We aim to provide a respectful and welcoming environment for everyone. Clients are expected to treat our staff, other clients and the premises with respect.

07

Calendar Bookings

Calendar links are provided as a convenience after completing an online booking. Clients are responsible for adding the appointment to their preferred calendar.

08

Changes to These Terms

House of Fades may update these terms when necessary. The latest version will always be made available on this website.
